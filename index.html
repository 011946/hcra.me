The Colophon

I.

HCRA is a one-person software establishment, founded and run by 9AA — an engineer who builds applications and games the way an old print shop once built a page: considered, typeset, and issued under a single mark. Nothing leaves the house half finished.

The name takes its shape from a printer's colophon, the small emblem once stamped inside a book to mark its house of origin. Software, here, is treated the same way — made once, made well, and made to last past the next update cycle.

One engineer. One mark. Every release.
