The House

An independent software house, founded in MMXXVI by 9AA.

HHCRA designs, builds, and publishes applications and games for iOS, Android, and Windows. Every title is created within the house, from the first sketch to the last update, and supported after release.

The house works to a simple rule: a title is released only when it is complete, dependable, and respectful of the people who use it.

Its first title, Windows Toolkit, is available now. More are in development.
